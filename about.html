Our Legacy

Shaping Quality Teacher Education Since 2014

We are Kerala's pioneer educational counselors, connecting ambitious teaching candidates to top NCTE-approved universities.

Global Education, Perinthalmanna was established with a singular objective: to provide 100% genuine and secure admissions guidance for professional teacher training programs like B.Ed and M.Ed. Over the past decade, we have successfully guided over 5,000 students from Malappuram, Kozhikode, Palakkad, and other parts of Kerala in securing degree seats in Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and state universities, ensuring complete validation for Kerala PSC postings.
